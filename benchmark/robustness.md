## Fields the taxonomy does not cover

A rule resting on one of these has a durability this harness does not predict, so they are named rather than counted.

`Contents`, `Hash`, `ObjectName`

## Where the field classes do not predict the loss

35 of the 51 credited rules were lost at a tier other than the first one that rewrites a field they require. The two directions mean different things.

**Lost later than the class reaches, 34 rules.** The tier rewrote a field the rule reads and the rule kept matching, because what was rewritten was the artifact the operator brought and the rule was reading something else in the same field class.

**Lost earlier than the class reaches, 1 rule.** A filter started applying. `required_groups` walks only the AND spine, so a detection removed by an exclusion the operator moved into is a loss no analysis of what a rule requires can see.

- Elevated System Shell Spawned From Uncommon Parent Location: reaches at `T3`, lost at `T2`
