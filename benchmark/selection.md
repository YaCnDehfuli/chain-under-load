## The control

The control rewrites `CurrentDirectory`, a field no rule in the technique-scoped selection requires, and has to leave `S-tag`, `S-aug` and `own` exactly where the baseline left them.

It moves nothing there, on all seven captures, on both readings: no rule loses a credit, gains one, stops firing or starts.

It does move something in the wide population, which is the evidence that it is a control rather than a formality. A mutation nothing reads can only ever pass.
